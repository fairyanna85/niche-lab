# 2026 search evidence → what the skill does about it

Figures come from vendor and survey data with different definitions; treat them as directional. Full links in `sources.md`.

| Evidence | Pitfall it implies | Skill response |
|---|---|---|
| Greenhouse: 18–22% of postings per quarter are not actively being filled; ~19% in Q2 2026 (M) | Applying to ghost postings; measuring demand on dead listings | Liveness score on every posting (rubric 08); ghosts excluded from demand size; employer list flags "watch, don't apply" |
| Agencies: 3% of listings, 10.8% of likely ghosts (M) | Agency mirrors inflate the market | Agency source penalty; employer/ATS copy preferred (rubric 06) |
| Gem 2026 (165M applications): 0.5% of applicants get offers; referrals convert at 11× inbound (M) | Cold applying at volume | Access map per employer and channel rule: referral path first, cold application last (rubric 15) |
| AI-written resumes look alike; 80% of hiring managers claim to spot them (survey) | Generic, machine-sounding resume | Authenticity gate (rubric 18), ledger-only facts, advocate test |
| Executive search: competence assumed, clarity and advocacy decide (E) | Vague positioning, too many targets | Niche portfolio with 2–3 active niches; one-sentence pitch per niche |
| Negotiators average +18.83%; 55% don't try (synthesis of 2024–25 studies); job-changers can reach up to 20% vs ~3.5% merit budgets (M/E) | Accepting the first number | Pay chapter per niche: band from coded postings, anchor, walk-away, counter script |
| Senior searches take months; interviews per hire rising (E/M) | Quitting a niche too early, or never pivoting | 6-to-12-month horizon, 30-day plan per niche, funnel tracker with diagnosis rules and park/activate triggers |
| Pitfall lists (Indeed, others): unclear goals, untailored resume, duties not achievements, no follow-up (E) | — | Niche verdicts, per-niche resume, X-Y-Z bullets, follow-up steps in the 30-day plan |
