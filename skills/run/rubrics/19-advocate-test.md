# Rubric 19 — Advocate test

A fresh subagent (prompt `prompts/advocate.md`) sees only the top third of the rendered resume for 30 seconds-equivalent (no scrolling, no other context) and completes: "This is a ___ who ___ for ___."
Pass = all three match: role family of the niche, one of the niche pitch's top-3 proof points, the niche's employer segment. Two of three = warn; fewer = fail → rewrite headline and profile.
Basis: at senior level, competence is assumed; what moves a search forward is a profile others can repeat and advocate for (IMD).
