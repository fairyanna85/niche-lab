# Rubric 17 — Bullet score (X-Y-Z)

Bock: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]". One point each:
1. Starts with a strong verb (not "Responsible for", "Helped", "Worked on").
2. X — an outcome, not an activity.
3. Y — a number, a baseline, or a named decision (a `[placeholder]` scores 0.5 and goes to the Confirm list).
4. Z — the method.
5. So-what — scope or context that makes the number meaningful (team, market, % of total).

Targets: average ≥4.0; no bullet <3; ≥2 bullets per role for the last 10 years; every role has a one-line company context and a scope line (people, budget or revenue managed).
