# Rubric 10 — Signal strength of a finding

| Label | Conditions | Wording allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | n ≥30 per compared group, Wilson 95% CI width ≤30 pts, p <0.05 (Fisher or seniority-adjusted Mantel–Haenszel), code kappa ≥0.6 | "X rose from a% to b%" |
| Moderate | n ≥15 per group, same direction in two eras or two segments, p <0.10 | "X appears to be rising" |
| Directional | anything else | counts only: "k of n postings" |

Never write "significant" for Moderate or Directional. Every chart caption states n. The then-vs-now chapter runs only with ≥15 archive postings; otherwise `change = null` with a hidden reason.
