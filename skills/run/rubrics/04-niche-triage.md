# Rubric 04 — Niche triage (phase 2, before screening)

A niche = role family × seniority band × employer segment × city. Generate 8–15 candidates by changing ONE dimension of the current profile at a time (adjacent role, adjacent segment, level up, level down, other city the candidate has a tie to, other side of the market) plus observed moves (where people with the same background went next, from public bios).

Score each candidate 0–10 before spending screen budget:
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Share of the candidate's grade ≥2 strengths that the niche's role family usually requires (judge from 3 sample postings) | 0–4 |
| Seniority alignment (same band 2, one band up or down 1, else 0) | 0–2 |
| Observed move exists (a public bio shows someone with similar background in this niche) | 0–2 |
| First search page shows ≥10 relevant live postings | 0–2 |

Screen every candidate scoring ≥5, in descending order, up to 15. Score <5 → `not_screened` with the reason.
City rule: only cities the candidate lives in, has worked in, or has a language/citizenship signal for. Never add a city on a guess.
