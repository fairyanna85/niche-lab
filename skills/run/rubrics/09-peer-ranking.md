# Rubric 09 — Peer ranking (supply side)

Sources, in order: company leadership/team pages, conference speaker bios, press releases announcing hires, podcast/guest bios, LinkedIn only when the page is readable without login. Store each bio's text and URL in `peers/raw/`. Minimum 10 profiles per niche; under 10 the peer chapter is hidden.
Who counts as a peer: currently holds a title inside the niche (role family + band + segment + city).

Score each profile 1–5 on five criteria:
| Criterion | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | 25% | single market, no team | regional, team ≤10 | global, team >10 or $50M+ number |
| Employer signal | 20% | unknown company | known in segment | category leader / top-tier brand |
| Trajectory | 20% | flat or lateral | one step up per 4+ yrs | one step up per ≤3 yrs |
| Impact evidence | 20% | duties only | some numbers | numbers on every role |
| External signal | 15% | none | occasional talks/posts | recurring speaker, author, board |

Weighted score → top cluster = top quartile (min 5 profiles); median group = middle 50%.
Report: per-criterion median vs top, plus feature diffs (headline pattern, years in band, prior employer types, credentials, languages, metrics density). Diffs where top − median ≥1 point are "what separates the top".
