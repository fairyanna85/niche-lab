# Rubric 12 — Niche qualification

| Verdict | Rule |
|---|---|
| Core | coverage ≥0.75, same seniority band, no blocker |
| Stretch | coverage 0.60–0.75, or one band up with coverage ≥0.75 |
| Pivot | coverage 0.40–0.60 with at least one rare edge (rubric 13 rarity), or a different role family |
| Rejected | coverage <0.40, a hard blocker, or failed volume in the screen |

Pay flag (never a reject, because the floor is inferred): niche median pay < 0.85 × estimated current pay → "pay step-down" chip and a line in the Confirm list.
Low-confidence flag: fewer than 30 coded "now" postings → statistical tests off, chapter shows counts, chip "low confidence".
